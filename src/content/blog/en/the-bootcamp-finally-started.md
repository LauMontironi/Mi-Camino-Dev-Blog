## 💻 First classes of the Bootcamp

So far we have revised **HTML and CSS**.

- What we have covered so far is not new to me thanks to my earlier studying.
- The teacher is brilliant and teaches you not to overcomplicate things: for example, for overlays in CSS, instead of `::after` pseudo-classes, he uses **separate divs with** `position: absolute/relative` and gradients, which is easier to understand and apply.

I am also exploring **Notion**, which is amazing for organising notes and pasting blocks of code.

The **final project of module 1** was to build a landing page with HTML and CSS. I submitted it and I am waiting for feedback.

---

## 🚀 Next challenge: JavaScript

- This is where the **hard part of the Bootcamp** begins, with functions, operators, loops and logic exercises.
- Personally, I feel like I am studying advanced maths: for example, counting even numbers from 1 to 1000, painting
  certain values in colours, or breaking out of loops with conditions.

I know it **makes practical sense**, even if right now it seems abstract: behind these calculations are **the processes of a shopping cart**, adding up prices, VAT, shipping, and so on.

In the meantime, I keep practising with Frontend Mentor challenges and now I am publishing them, although with a lot of embarrassment 😅.

## 📊 How I am organising my learning

I have mentally divided the content into parts:

1. **Variables and data types**
2. **Operators and basic logic** (addition, subtraction, comparison, logical operators)
3. **Interacting with the DOM** (when the user clicks something, colours change, a message appears…)

Meanwhile, I keep doing practical exercises on my own, experimenting with **Tailwind** (I still find it hard 😅) and applying basic JS to mini projects.
