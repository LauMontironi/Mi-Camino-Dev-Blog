Hello again! today I am here to tell you what happens when the screen goes off and the mind collapses (mine)

🌪️ The "information overload" loop

Before the holidays, I went into a dangerous mode: every free second went
on a YouTube video, a FastAPI tutorial or trying to replicate SQL classes
without a break.

Conclusion: Mental oversaturation, level one thousand. 🤯

The result: The Christmas holidays arrived, I decided (finally) to switch
off... and when I came back, boom! I felt like I had forgotten everything.

🌑 The black cloud and impostor syndrome

My 2026 did not start with fireworks. I found myself trapped in a black cloud of dust:

I am finding it incredibly hard to get back into the rhythm.

Negative thoughts ("the market is hostile", "I am not cut out for this") beat the positive ones hands down.

I lack perspective: if someone told me my own story (family, children,
bootcamp, work), I would say: "Wow, that is incredible!". But when it is
about me, I do not feel deserving of any credentials at all. 🏷️🚫

🛑 Stopping is not failing

I have realised that my mind needed those holidays for what they are:
holidays. The IT world is immense and sometimes we convince ourselves that
if we stop for a second, the train leaves without us. But the reality is
that if we do not stop, the engine burns out.

🌈 Why do I choose to carry on?

Even though today I feel vulnerable and the world seems a difficult place, I choose to keep trying.

Because I am proud to be here.

Because I have a family that supports me.

Because, even if today I see "clouds", I know that everything I learned is still underneath them.

Sometimes, re-programming our own mind is harder than any line of code in JavaScript. 💛

And with that, I am closing this very necessary reflection. My next post
will be about the process, about the "hows" and the "what I am learning",
unless my mind decides otherwise and asks me for another pause to
reconnect! 😉
